What KonumOS does

KonumOS is a marketing workflow product preview for agencies. It organizes strategy memory, research hypotheses, draft variations, approval and export packages. The person operating it owns the positioning and the decision to publish.

How the workflow is organized

  1. Strategy Memory: record the audience, positioning, brand rules and the source material that work should follow.
  2. Evidence Engine: review messaging and develop questions or hypotheses to test. A hypothesis is not a demonstrated result.
  3. Execution Studio: prepare variations for review. Brand-rule checks and human approval precede an export package.
  4. Signal Radar: review recorded performance and the evidence available before deciding what to change.

The main site’s workflow describes these stations. The public Message Map page offers a starting point for reviewing homepage messaging.

What the preview does not promise

The animated demos and weekly activity figures on the main site use simulated data. They do not establish real customer activity, campaign performance, time savings or revenue.

KonumOS v1 prepares packages for human use. It does not publish directly into advertising, email or social accounts. A scheduled or exported package is not evidence that it was published, that anyone saw it or that it produced sales.

The pilot figures on the main page are assumptions and pilot targets, not a finalized public price list or a profit guarantee. The product does not guarantee sales, conversion, search ranking or inclusion in AI answers.
